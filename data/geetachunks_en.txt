### **Chapter 1: Arjuna’s Dilemma**

**[Chapter 1: Arjuna’s Dilemma | Topic: The Universal Dilemma | Verse: 1.1]** The narrative begins as King Dhritarashtra asks for an account of the battle. You, Arjuna, asked Me to place your chariot between the two armies, but upon seeing your kin, you were paralyzed by sorrow, spoke of the evils of war, and sat down on the back seat of the chariot with a mind overwhelmed.

---

### **Chapter 2: Spiritual Knowledge**

**[Chapter 2: Spiritual Knowledge | Topic: The spirit is eternal, body is transitory | Verse: 2.16]** Understand that the invisible Spirit is eternal, while the physical body and visible world are but transitory; this is the truth seen by the seers. The Spirit pervading this universe is indestructible and cannot be slain.

**[Chapter 2: Spiritual Knowledge | Topic: Death and Reincarnation of the soul | Verse: 2.22]** Just as a person discards old garments for new ones, the living entity acquires new bodies after casting away the old. The Spirit is eternal, all-pervading, and unchanging—it cannot be cut by weapons or burned by fire.

**[Chapter 2: Spiritual Knowledge | Topic: Duty of a warrior | Verse: 2.31]** Considering your duty, do not waver, for there is nothing more auspicious for a warrior than a righteous war against evil. To refuse this duty is to incur sin and lose your reputation.

**[Chapter 2: Spiritual Knowledge | Topic: Importance of Karma-yoga, the selfless action | Verse: 2.39]** I impart to you the wisdom of Karma-yoga; by this, you shall be freed from the bondage of your actions. In this path, no effort is lost, and even a little practice protects you from the great fear of birth and death.

**[Chapter 2: Spiritual Knowledge | Topic: Material and spiritual aspects of life | Verse: 2.42]** Do not be like the misguided who delight in rituals for heavenly enjoyment; their minds are dominated by material desires. Rise above the three modes of nature and the pairs of opposites to be Self-conscious.

**[Chapter 2: Spiritual Knowledge | Topic: Theory and practice of Karma-yoga | Verse: 2.47]** You have control over your duty, but no claim over the results. Work to the best of your ability with a mind attached to Me, remaining calm in both success and failure—this calmness of mind is yoga.

**[Chapter 2: Spiritual Knowledge | Topic: Marks of a Self-realized person | Verse: 2.55]** An enlightened person is one who is completely free from all desires of the mind and is satisfied with the Eternal Being alone. Such a sage is unperturbed by sorrow and does not crave pleasure.

**[Chapter 2: Spiritual Knowledge | Topic: Dangers of unrestrained senses | Verse: 2.62]** Thinking of sense objects leads to attachment, then desire, and then anger. Anger leads to delusion, which destroys reasoning and causes one to fall from the right path.

**[Chapter 2: Spiritual Knowledge | Topic: Peace through sense control and Self-knowledge | Verse: 2.64]** A disciplined person, free from likes and dislikes, attains tranquility. This superconscious state leads to peace, and even at the end of life, it allows one to attain Nirvana.

---

### **Chapter 3: Path of Selfless Service, Karma-yoga**

**[Chapter 3: Path of Selfless Service | Topic: Why one should serve others? | Verse: 3.3]** I have stated a twofold path: Self-knowledge for the contemplative and the path of unselfish work for others. No one can remain actionless, for nature drives all to act; but those who engage in selfless service are superior.

**[Chapter 3: Path of Selfless Service | Topic: The first commandment of the creator | Verse: 3.10]** The creator established that humans should prosper through selfless service. By helping each other—you and the celestial controllers—you shall attain the supreme goal.

**[Chapter 3: Path of Selfless Service | Topic: Leaders should set an example | Verse: 3.19]** Always perform your duty efficiently and without attachment, for the world follows the standard set by noble persons. Even I engage in action relentlessly to prevent the confusion and destruction of these worlds.

**[Chapter 3: Path of Selfless Service | Topic: All works are the works of Nature | Verse: 3.27]** All work is done by the energy and power of nature; only the deluded person assumes they are the doer. The wise, knowing this, do not become attached to work.

**[Chapter 3: Path of Selfless Service | Topic: Two stumbling blocks on the path | Verse: 3.34]** Likes and dislikes for sense objects are the two major stumbling blocks on your path of Self-realization. It is better to perform your own natural work, even if inferior, than unnatural work.

**[Chapter 3: Path of Selfless Service | Topic: Lust is the origin of sin | Verse: 3.37]** Lust, born of passion, is the great devil and the enemy that veils Self-knowledge. It sits in your senses, mind, and intellect, deluding you by covering the truth.

**[Chapter 3: Path of Selfless Service | Topic: How to control lust | Verse: 3.41]** By controlling your senses first, you must kill this mighty enemy that destroys Self-knowledge and Self-realization.

---

### **Chapter 4: Path of Renunciation with Self-Knowledge**

**[Chapter 4: Path of Renunciation | Topic: Karma-yoga is an ancient law | Verse: 4.1]** I taught this eternal science to King Vivasvan in ancient times; though it was lost, I reveal it to you now because you are My sincere devotee and friend.

**[Chapter 4: Path of Renunciation | Topic: The purpose of incarnation of God | Verse: 4.7]** Whenever righteousness declines and unrighteousness predominates, I manifest Myself to protect the good, transform the wicked, and establish world order.

**[Chapter 4: Path of Renunciation | Topic: Path of worship and prayer | Verse: 4.11]** I fulfill the desires of people in accordance with the motive with which they worship Me. I created the four divisions of society based on aptitude and vocation.

**[Chapter 4: Path of Renunciation | Topic: Attached, detached, and forbidden action | Verse: 4.17]** Understanding the true nature of action is difficult; one must know the difference between selfish, selfless, and forbidden action.

**[Chapter 4: Path of Renunciation | Topic: A Karma-yogi is not subject to the Karmic laws | Verse: 4.18]** One who sees inaction in action and action in inaction is wise. Such a person, free from desires and attachment to the fruits of work, incurs no Karmic reaction.

**[Chapter 4: Path of Renunciation | Topic: Different types of spiritual practices | Verse: 4.25]** Some perform worship, others study scriptures, and some practice breathing or yogic exercises; all who perform such selfless service are purified by their sacrifice.

**[Chapter 4: Path of Renunciation | Topic: A superior spiritual practice | Verse: 4.33]** The acquisition of Self-knowledge is superior to any material gift, for all actions culminate in knowledge. Acquire this from a Self-realized guru through reverence and service.

**[Chapter 4: Path of Renunciation | Topic: Spiritual knowledge is revealed to a Karma-yogi | Verse: 4.37]** The fire of Self-knowledge reduces all bonds of Karma to ashes. Having gained this knowledge, one attains supreme peace.

**[Chapter 4: Path of Renunciation | Topic: Knowledge, Karma-yoga are needed for Nirvana | Verse: 4.41]** Work does not bind one whose doubts are destroyed by Self-knowledge; therefore, cut your doubts with the sword of knowledge and resort to Karma-yoga.

---

### **Chapter 5: Path of Renunciation**

**[Chapter 5: Path of Renunciation | Topic: The path of Self-knowledge | Verse: 5.2]** Both the path of Self-knowledge and selfless service lead to the supreme goal, but selfless service is superior.

**[Chapter 5: Path of Renunciation | Topic: Both paths lead to the Supreme | Verse: 5.4]** The ignorant see these paths as different, but the wise know that mastering one brings the benefits of both. A Karma-yogi whose mind is pure and who sees the Eternal Being in all is not bound.

**[Chapter 5: Path of Renunciation | Topic: A Karma-yogi works for God only | Verse: 5.10]** One who works as an offering to the Lord remains untouched by sin, like a lotus leaf that never gets wet. They perform actions only for self-purification.

**[Chapter 5: Path of Renunciation | Topic: The path of Self-knowledge | Verse: 5.16]** Spiritual knowledge destroys ignorance and reveals the Supreme. Those whose minds are merged with the Source do not take birth again.

**[Chapter 5: Path of Renunciation | Topic: Additional marks of an enlightened person | Verse: 5.18]** An enlightened person looks at all beings—a priest, an outcast, or an animal—with an equal eye. They find happiness with the Eternal Being within and attain Nirvana.

**[Chapter 5: Path of Renunciation | Topic: Path of meditation and contemplation | Verse: 5.27]** A sage is liberated by renouncing material enjoyments, controlling the senses and mind through yogic techniques, and making salvation the primary goal.

---

### **Chapter 6: Path of Meditation**

**[Chapter 6: Path of Meditation | Topic: A Karma-yogi is also a renunciant | Verse: 6.1]** One who performs duty without seeking its fruit is both a renunciant and a Karma-yogi. No one becomes a yogi without renouncing selfish motives.

**[Chapter 6: Path of Meditation | Topic: A definition of yoga and yogi | Verse: 6.3]** Yogic perfection is reached when one has no desire for sensual pleasures or attachment to the fruits of work.

**[Chapter 6: Path of Meditation | Topic: Mind is both a friend and an enemy | Verse: 6.5]** Elevate yourself by your own mind; it is your friend when controlled, but an enemy when it is not.

**[Chapter 6: Path of Meditation | Topic: Techniques of meditation | Verse: 6.10]** Sit in solitude, fix your mind on God, and practice meditation in a clean spot with an erect spine for self-purification. Moderation in eating and sleeping is essential for this yoga.

**[Chapter 6: Path of Meditation | Topic: Who is a yogi? | Verse: 6.27]** A yogi with a tranquil mind and controlled desires enjoys the infinite bliss of contact with God. The best yogi regards every being as himself.

**[Chapter 6: Path of Meditation | Topic: Two methods to subdue the mind | Verse: 6.35]** Though the mind is restless, it can be subdued by constant spiritual practice and detachment.

**[Chapter 6: Path of Meditation | Topic: Destination of unsuccessful yogi | Verse: 6.41]** The unsuccessful yogi is reborn in a pious family, regains previous knowledge, and strives again for perfection.

**[Chapter 6: Path of Meditation | Topic: Who is the best yogi? | Verse: 6.47]** I consider the yogi-devotee who lovingly contemplates Me with supreme faith to be the best of all.

---

### **Chapter 7: Self-knowledge and Enlightenment**

**[Chapter 7: Self-knowledge and Enlightenment | Topic: Definitions of matter, consciousness, and spirit | Verse: 7.4]** My lower nature is eightfold material energy, but My higher nature is the Spirit by which this universe is sustained.

**[Chapter 7: Self-knowledge and Enlightenment | Topic: The Supreme spirit is the basis of everything | Verse: 7.7]** Everything in the universe is strung on Me like jewels on a thread. I am the life in all living beings and the eternal seed of all creatures.

**[Chapter 7: Self-knowledge and Enlightenment | Topic: Who seeks God? | Verse: 7.14]** Only those who surrender to Me can cross My divine illusive power (Maya). The four types who seek Me are the distressed, the seeker of knowledge, the seeker of wealth, and the enlightened.

**[Chapter 7: Self-knowledge and Enlightenment | Topic: God can be seen in any image of worship | Verse: 7.21]** I make the faith steady in whoever desires to worship any deity with faith. The ignorant do not know My transcendental form and assume I take human form as they do.

---

### **Chapter 8: The Eternal Being, God**

**[Chapter 8: The Eternal Being, God | Topic: Definitions of supreme spirit, spirit, individual soul | Verse: 8.3]** The immutable Spirit is God; the power of God that causes manifestation is Karma. I am the Supersoul residing inside every cell as the supreme controller.

**[Chapter 8: The Eternal Being, God | Topic: Theory of reincarnation and Karma | Verse: 8.5]** One who remembers Me while leaving the body at death attains Me. Whatever object you remember at the end of life, that you shall achieve.

**[Chapter 8: The Eternal Being, God | Topic: A simple method of God-realization | Verse: 8.7]** Always remember Me and do your duty; you shall certainly attain Me if your mind and intellect are focused on Me.

**[Chapter 8: The Eternal Being, God | Topic: Attain salvation by meditating on God | Verse: 8.12]** By controlling the senses and meditating on Me while uttering OM, one attains the supreme abode.

**[Chapter 8: The Eternal Being, God | Topic: Everything in the creation is cyclic | Verse: 8.17]** A day of the creator lasts billions of years; beings emerge at day and merge back at night in a continuous cycle.

**[Chapter 8: The Eternal Being, God | Topic: Two basic paths of departure after death | Verse: 8.24]** There is a path of light leading to salvation and a path of darkness leading to rebirth.

**[Chapter 8: The Eternal Being, God | Topic: Spiritual knowledge leads to salvation | Verse: 8.27]** Knowing these paths, a yogi is not bewildered; be steadfast in your union with Me.

---

### **Chapter 9: Supreme Knowledge and the Big Mystery**

**[Chapter 9: Supreme Knowledge | Topic: Knowledge of the supreme is the biggest mystery | Verse: 9.2]** This Self-knowledge is the king of all knowledge and the most secret mystery. All beings depend on Me, yet I am independent of them.

**[Chapter 9: Supreme Knowledge | Topic: The theory of evolution and involution | Verse: 9.7]** I create the multitude of beings again and again through My material nature at the start of each cycle.

**[Chapter 9: Supreme Knowledge | Topic: Everything is a manifestation of God | Verse: 9.16]** I am the ritual, the sacrifice, the supporter, the father, and the mother of the universe. I am the sacred 'OM,' the goal, the refuge, and the immutable seed.

**[Chapter 9: Supreme Knowledge | Topic: Attaining salvation by devotional love | Verse: 9.22]** I personally take care of the material and spiritual welfare of those who always remember and adore Me.

**[Chapter 9: Supreme Knowledge | Topic: The Lord accepts and eats the offering of love | Verse: 9.26]** I accept even a leaf, a flower, or water if offered with devotion by a pure-hearted seeker.

**[Chapter 9: Supreme Knowledge | Topic: There is no unforgivable sinner | Verse: 9.30]** Even the most sinful, if they resolve to worship Me with single-minded devotion, must be regarded as a saint. Sincere repentance burns all sins.

**[Chapter 9: Supreme Knowledge | Topic: The path of devotional love is easier | Verse: 9.32]** Anyone who surrenders to My will with loving devotion can attain the supreme abode.

---

### **Chapter 10: Manifestation of the Absolute**

**[Chapter 10: Manifestation of the Absolute | Topic: God is the origin of everything | Verse: 10.2]** I am the origin of all celestial controllers and great sages. All diverse qualities in humans, from intelligence to fear, arise from Me.

**[Chapter 10: Manifestation of the Absolute | Topic: God gives spiritual knowledge to His devotees | Verse: 10.10]** I give knowledge to those who adore Me, and I destroy their darkness of ignorance with the lamp of spiritual knowledge.

**[Chapter 10: Manifestation of the Absolute | Topic: Everything is a manifestation of the Absolute | Verse: 10.20]** I am the Spirit abiding in the inner psyche of all beings; I am their beginning, middle, and end.

**[Chapter 10: Manifestation of the Absolute | Topic: A brief description of divine manifestations | Verse: 10.25]** Among mountains, I am the Himalaya; among words, I am the cosmic sound 'OM'; among warriors, I am Rama; and I am all-devouring death.

**[Chapter 10: Manifestation of the Absolute | Topic: Creation is a small part of the Absolute | Verse: 10.41]** Everything endowed with glory and power is a manifestation of but a small fraction of My splendor.

---

### **Chapter 11: Vision of the Universal Form**

**[Chapter 11: Vision of the Universal Form | Topic: The vision of God is the ultimate aim of a seeker | Verse: 11.1]** You asked to see My universal form, and I granted you the divine eye to behold My majestic power.

**[Chapter 11: Vision of the Universal Form | Topic: Lord Krishna shows His cosmic form | Verse: 11.9]** I revealed My form containing the entire universe, blazing with the splendor of thousands of suns.

**[Chapter 11: Vision of the Universal Form | Topic: Arjuna is frightened to see the Cosmic form | Verse: 11.24]** Seeing My mouths like the fires of dissolution, you were frightened. I am death, the mighty destroyer, come to destroy these warriors.

**[Chapter 11: Vision of the Universal Form | Topic: We are only a divine instrument | Verse: 11.33]** These warriors are already destroyed by Me; get up and be My instrument to attain glory. All battles are Mine, and I do everything through your body.

**[Chapter 11: Vision of the Universal Form | Topic: God can be seen by devotional love | Verse: 11.54]** I can be known in essence and reached only through single-minded devotion.

---

### **Chapter 12: Path of Devotion**

**[Chapter 12: Path of Devotion | Topic: Worship a personal or impersonal God? | Verse: 12.1]** While those who worship the unmanifest Absolute also attain Me, it is much more difficult for embodied beings.

**[Chapter 12: Path of Devotion | Topic: Reasons for worshipping a personal God | Verse: 12.6]** I swiftly become the savior of those who worship Me as a personal God and offer all actions to Me.

**[Chapter 12: Path of Devotion | Topic: The four paths to God | Verse: 12.8]** Focus on Me, or practice any discipline that suits you; even working as My instrument without selfish motives leads to perfection.

**[Chapter 12: Path of Devotion | Topic: Karma-yoga is the best Path | Verse: 12.12]** Renunciation of attachment to the fruits of work is better than meditation; peace follows such renunciation immediately.

**[Chapter 12: Path of Devotion | Topic: The attributes of a devotee | Verse: 12.13]** My dear devotee is friendly, compassionate, free from "I" and "my," and even-minded in pleasure and pain.

---

### **Chapter 13: Creation and the Creator**

**[Chapter 13: Creation and the Creator | Topic: The theory of creation | Verse: 13.1]** The physical body is the creation, and the one who knows it is the creator. Understanding both is true spiritual knowledge.

**[Chapter 13: Creation and the Creator | Topic: The fourfold noble truth as means of Nirvana | Verse: 13.7]** Humility, nonviolence, honesty, and unswerving devotion to Me are the means of acquiring knowledge.

**[Chapter 13: Creation and the Creator | Topic: A description of the supreme spirit, spirit, and jiva | Verse: 13.19]** Material nature and the Spiritual Being are both beginningless. The Spirit in the body is the witness and the Supreme Self.

**[Chapter 13: Creation and the Creator | Topic: Attributes of the spirit | Verse: 13.31]** The eternal Supersoul, though dwelling in the body, does not do anything and is not tainted, just as space is not tainted by its subtlety.

---

### **Chapter 14: Three Modes (Gunas) of Nature**

**[Chapter 14: Three Modes of Nature | Topic: Beings are born from the union of spirit and matter | Verse: 14.3]** Material nature is the mother, and I am the life-giving father.

**[Chapter 14: Three Modes of Nature | Topic: How three modes of material nature bind us | Verse: 14.5]** Goodness, Passion, and Ignorance fetter the eternal soul to the body. Goodness attaches one to happiness; Passion to action; and Ignorance to laziness.

**[Chapter 14: Three Modes of Nature | Topic: Three modes are also the vehicles of transmigration | Verse: 14.14]** One who dies in goodness goes to heaven; in passion, they are reborn attached to action; in ignorance, they are reborn as lower creatures.

**[Chapter 14: Three Modes of Nature | Topic: Attain Nirvana after rising above three modes | Verse: 14.19]** When you perceive no doer other than these modes and know the Supreme beyond them, you attain salvation.

**[Chapter 14: Three Modes of Nature | Topic: Bonds of three modes can be cut by devotional love | Verse: 14.26]** One who serves Me with unswerving love rises above these modes and becomes fit for Nirvana.

---

### **Chapter 15: The Supreme Being**

**[Chapter 15: The Supreme Being | Topic: Creation is like a tree created by of Maya | Verse: 15.1]** This cosmos is like an eternal banyan tree with roots above and branches below.

**[Chapter 15: The Supreme Being | Topic: Cut the tree of attachment to attain salvation | Verse: 15.3]** Cut the roots of this tree—ego and desire—with the ax of Self-knowledge and detachment to reach the supreme abode.

**[Chapter 15: The Supreme Being | Topic: Spirit is the essence of everything | Verse: 15.12]** I am the light in the sun, the energy supporting all beings, and the digestive fire within you. I am seated in your inner psyche as memory and knowledge.

**[Chapter 15: The Supreme Being | Topic: Supreme spirit, spirit and the created beings | Verse: 15.16]** I am the Supreme Being who transcends both the temporal world and the eternal Spirit.

### **Chapter 16: Divine and the Demonic Qualities**

**[Chapter 16: Divine and Demonic Qualities | Topic: A list of divine qualities to be cultivated | Verse: 16.1]** Cultivate the twenty-six divine virtues, like fearlessness, charity, and honesty, which lead to salvation.

**[Chapter 16: Divine and Demonic Qualities | Topic: A list of demonic qualities to be given up | Verse: 16.4]** Hypocrisy, pride, and ignorance lead to bondage. Demons believe the world is without God or order and are born for its destruction.

**[Chapter 16: Divine and Demonic Qualities | Topic: Lust, anger, and greed are the three gates to hell | Verse: 16.21]** These three are the gates of hell leading to bondage; you must give them up to attain the supreme abode.

**[Chapter 16: Divine and Demonic Qualities | Topic: One must follow the scripture | Verse: 16.24]** Let scripture be your authority in determining what should be done.

### **Chapter 17: Threefold Faith**

**[Chapter 17: Threefold Faith | Topic: Three types of faith | Verse: 17.2]** Your faith is of three kinds: Goodness, passion, and ignorance. You are known by your faith and become whatever you contemplate with it.

**[Chapter 17: Threefold Faith | Topic: Three types of food | Verse: 17.8]** Food in goodness promotes health and joy; food in passion causes pain and disease; and food in ignorance is stale or impure.

**[Chapter 17: Threefold Faith | Topic: Austerity of thought, word, and deed | Verse: 17.14]** Austerity consists of purity and nonviolence in deeds, truthful and beneficial speech, and serenity of mind.

**[Chapter 17: Threefold Faith | Topic: Threefold name of God | Verse: 17.23]** 'OM TAT SAT' is the threefold name of God; all acts of sacrifice and charity should commence with 'OM'.

---

### **Chapter 18: Attaining Liberation by Giving Up Ego**

**[Chapter 18: Attaining Liberation | Topic: Definition of renunciation and sacrifice | Verse: 18.2]** Renunciation is giving up work for personal profit; sacrifice is giving up attachment to the fruits of all work.

**[Chapter 18: Attaining Liberation | Topic: Three types of sacrifice | Verse: 18.9]** Obligatory work performed as duty, renouncing attachment to the result, is sacrifice in the mode of goodness.

**[Chapter 18: Attaining Liberation | Topic: Three types of spiritual knowledge | Verse: 18.20]** Knowledge in goodness sees a single immutable Reality in all beings.

**[Chapter 18: Attaining Liberation | Topic: Attain salvation by duty, discipline, devotion | Verse: 18.45]** You can attain perfection by worshipping the Supreme Being through your natural duty.

**[Chapter 18: Attaining Liberation | Topic: Karmic bondage and the free will | Verse: 18.61]** The Lord causes all beings to evolve like puppets mounted on a machine of Karma. Karma is the eternal justice from which there is no escape.

**[Chapter 18: Attaining Liberation | Topic: Path of surrender is the ultimate path to God | Verse: 18.66]** Set aside all doership and attachment; just surrender to My Will. I shall liberate you from all sins. Do not grieve.

**[Chapter 18: Attaining Liberation | Topic: Both spiritual knowledge and action are needed | Verse: 18.78]** Wherever the Lord of yoga and the weapons of duty are present, there will be prosperity, victory, and morality.
